PRIVACY POLICY — Sailing Calculator

Last updated: September 30, 2026

This Privacy Policy describes how the Sailing Calculator mobile application
("the App") handles information when you use it. The App is developed and
maintained by an individual developer:

Developer: Grzegorz Koralewski
Contact e-mail: koralewski.g@gmail.com

By using the App, you agree to the collection and use of information in
accordance with this policy. If you do not agree with this policy, please
do not use the App.


1. INFORMATION WE DO NOT COLLECT

The App does not require you to create an account. We do not collect,
store, transmit, or have access to your name, e-mail address, phone
number, or any other personally identifiable information.

The App does not use analytics, crash-reporting, or tracking SDKs, and
does not include advertising or third-party advertising SDKs.


2. LOCATION DATA

The App requests access to your device's location (fine and/or coarse
location permission) in order to provide its core functionality: showing
your current position, calculating tacking/upwind sailing routes, and
calibrating navigation features relative to your position and heading in
real time.

- Your location is used only momentarily, on your device, to power the
  App's live on-screen calculations and map display.
- The App does not save, log, or store your location, and does not
  transmit it to the developer or to any server, because the developer
  does not operate any backend server at all.
- The App may rely on Google Play Services / Google's location APIs on
  Android to obtain positioning data from your device's GPS and other
  sensors. As part of resolving your position, your approximate location
  may be processed by Google in accordance with Google's own Privacy
  Policy (https://policies.google.com/privacy). This processing happens
  at the operating-system level and is outside the developer's control;
  it is disclosed here for transparency, even though the developer itself
  never receives, stores, or has access to this data.
- When you use the App's wind/weather features, your current coordinates
  (or the coordinates of a point you selected on the map) are sent to the
  third-party weather service Open-Meteo (open-meteo.com) solely in order
  to request current or forecast wind direction for that location. This
  request does not include your name, device identifiers, or any account
  information, because the App has none to send — only the geographic
  coordinates needed to answer "what is the wind doing here". Open-Meteo
  does not require or use an API key or account for this request. This
  data is governed by Open-Meteo's own privacy practices
  (https://open-meteo.com/en/terms), independently of this App.
- You can disable location access at any time through your device
  settings. Core features of the App will not work without location
  access, since the App is built around real-time positioning.


3. LOCAL STORAGE ON YOUR DEVICE

The App saves some information locally on your own device so that it
keeps working the way you left it between sessions. This includes:

- Your app settings and preferences (e.g., display and calibration
  options).
- Manually planned routes you create in the App (start/destination
  coordinates and route points).
- Information about which map area you have downloaded for offline use,
  and the downloaded map tiles themselves.

None of this locally stored data is ever transmitted to the developer or
to any third party — it stays on your device, under your control. You can
delete it at any time by clearing the App's storage in your device
settings or by uninstalling the App.


4. INTERNET ACCESS

The App uses internet access for two purposes only:

- Downloading offline map tiles from third-party map tile providers, as
  described in Section 3.
- Requesting wind direction/forecast data from Open-Meteo, as described
  in Section 2.

No other data is sent over the internet by the App.


5. DATA SHARING WITH THIRD PARTIES

We do not collect any personal user data ourselves, so we have no such
data to sell, rent, or trade. The only outside parties technically
involved in the App's operation, and what they receive, are:

- Map tile providers — receive requests for map imagery for the area you
  choose to download; do not receive your identity or any personal data.
- Open-Meteo — receives geographic coordinates only, to return wind
  data for that location, as described in Section 2.
- Google Play Services — may process your approximate location at the
  operating-system level as described in Section 2, subject to Google's
  own Privacy Policy.

The developer itself does not receive, store, or further share any data
from or with any of these parties.


6. DATA RETENTION

The developer does not operate a backend server and does not collect,
receive, or retain any user data remotely. Locally stored data (settings,
manually planned routes, offline map tiles — see Section 3) remains only
on your device, under your control, until you clear it or uninstall the
App.

age required by your local law) and we do not knowingly collect data from
children. If you believe a child has provided us with information, please
contact us at the e-mail address above so we can address it.

Google Play Services, as described in Section 2, is governed by Google's
